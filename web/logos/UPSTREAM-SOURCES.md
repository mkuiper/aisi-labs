Marks belong to their respective owners. Their display identifies the tools
available for learning and does not imply sponsorship or endorsement. Other
tool cards use labelled badges where a suitable logo has not been verified.
The `coloured/` copies use native colour variants where available, plus display
colours for monochrome marks. The original source files above are retained
unchanged. `make_coloured.py` rebuilds the additional colour copies.
